AI and Garment Structure — Chinese Edition

《AI与服装结构：从图像到成衣的探索》中文版

本书探索AI时代品牌创立的新路径，从AI视觉概念出发，通过结构判断、版型转换、工艺剖析与产品推演，将创意图像转化为具有品牌语言和技术实现体系的服装系统。

This book explores a new path for fashion brand creation in the age of AI. Starting from AI-generated visual concepts, it transforms creative images into a fashion system with a distinctive brand language and technical realization process through structure analysis, pattern development, construction breakdown, and product projection.

Early Fashion Publication Archive, 1987

《深圳女装设计》｜1987年出版

本书创作于1985年，是中国服装设计发展早期的专业探索。由于当时国内时尚资料极为有限，设计与版型结合的专业书籍几乎空白，作者通过大量手绘设计图、结构图与纸样分析，将服装设计理念与版型技术进行系统结合。

Shenzhen Women's Fashion Design | Published in 1987

Created in 1985, this book represents an early exploration of fashion design and pattern development in China. At a time when fashion resources were extremely limited, it combined hand-drawn design illustrations, garment structures, and pattern analysis to connect creative ideas with technical realization.
