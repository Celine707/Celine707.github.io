About Celine Chen · Hongxia

A Fashion Journey Across China and America for Over Four Decades

01｜中国 · 1980年代—1990年代

1980s–1990s · China
Fashion Designer · Brand Founder · Educator

陈红霞（Celine Chen）毕业于中央工艺美术学院（现清华大学美术学院）服装设计专业。她的职业生涯始于中国时尚产业快速发展的重要时期，从服装设计师，到创立个人品牌、经营服装企业、参与时装发布，并长期从事高校教学与专业人才培养。

设计、生产、商业与教育的多重经历，使她形成了从创意、结构、工艺到产品实现的完整服装体系。

Celine Chen graduated from the Fashion Design Department of the Academy of Arts & Design, Tsinghua University, formerly the Central Academy of Arts and Design. Her fashion career began during an important period of China’s fashion industry development. She worked as a fashion designer, founded her own fashion brands and company, participated in fashion shows, and devoted herself to fashion education and professional training.

Her experience across design, production, business, and education shaped a comprehensive understanding of fashion — from creative concepts to structure, construction, and final realization.

02｜纽约 · 高级定制之路

New York Couture Journey
Senior Couture Pattern Maker · Luxury Fashion Structure

来到纽约后，陈红霞继续深入服装结构与高级成衣领域，长期参与美国高端时装品牌的开发工作。她将设计师的创意草图转化为可实现的服装结构，通过立体裁剪、版型调整与工艺控制，让设计从概念走向真实作品。

多年纽约高级时装行业经历，使她更加确认：真正的服装设计不仅存在于图像之中，更存在于人体、材料、结构与制作过程中。

After moving to New York, Celine Chen continued her journey in garment structure and couture development, working with luxury fashion brands and transforming designers’ concepts into real garments through draping, pattern development, fitting, and construction.

Her years of experience in the New York fashion industry strengthened her belief that true fashion design does not exist only in images — it lives through the relationship between the human body, materials, structure, and craftsmanship.

03｜1983年至今 · 服装教育与人机协同

1983–Present · Fashion Education
Teaching · Publications · Human–AI Collaboration

从设计实践到教育传播，陈红霞始终关注服装知识体系的建立与传承。她长期从事服装结构、立体裁剪、版型工艺及设计方法的教学，将多年行业经验转化为系统课程，帮助更多设计师理解服装背后的结构逻辑。

随着人工智能时代的到来，她开始探索AI与服装专业经验的结合，通过书籍写作、案例研究与人机协同实验，寻找未来服装设计与教育的新方向。

From professional practice to fashion education, Celine Chen has continuously focused on building and sharing systematic fashion knowledge. Through teaching garment structure, draping, pattern making, and design methodology, she transforms decades of industry experience into practical learning systems for designers.

With the arrival of the AI era, she began exploring the collaboration between artificial intelligence and professional fashion expertise through book writing, case studies, and human–AI experiments, searching for new possibilities in the future of fashion design and education.
